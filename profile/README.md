# Coastguard North Shore

A volunteer unit of Coastguard New Zealand, based at Browns Bay on Auckland's
North Shore. Our crews are on call 24 hours a day, 365 days a year, aboard
North Shore Rescue.

This organisation holds the software that supports the unit:

- **Crew roster**: crew days, market duties, hours and MOSS drills ([roster.cgns.co.nz](https://roster.cgns.co.nz))
- **QC training**: self-paced study for the Qualified Crew assessment ([qc-trainer.cgns.co.nz](https://qc-trainer.cgns.co.nz))
- **Website**: [cgns.co.nz](https://cgns.co.nz)

Built and maintained by unit volunteers. The repositories are private.

**Emergency at sea:** VHF Channel 16 · *500 from your mobile · 0508 RESCUE (737 283)
